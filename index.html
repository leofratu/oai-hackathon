Why WebMCP

The page owns the model. The agent works through contracts.

inspect_uncertain_samples returns ranked candidates with probabilities and entropy. The agent does not scrape cards or infer control meaning from the layout.

queue_label_review can request labels but cannot assign them. train_confirmed_batch rejects unreviewed predictions, and configuration changes remain pending until a person accepts them.
