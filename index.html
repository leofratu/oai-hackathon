Why this needs WebMCP

A protocol, not pixel automation.

A click-driving agent would have to guess at pixels and scrape visual state. Here it discovers explicit capabilities, receives typed evidence, and can only make the bounded changes each tool permits. Every mutation appears in the page; you keep the pencil and the final say.
